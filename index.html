How it works

1Post the jobDescribe the work and set your price. Your exact address stays hidden until a technician accepts.
2Pay — held securelyYou fund the job up front; Stripe holds the money and it's only released when you sign off — never handed over early.
3A technician acceptsVerified, licence-checked technicians nearby accept and arrive within 4 hours, with before & after proof photos.
4Sign off, covered 15 daysApprove the finished work to release payment. Something wrong within 15 days? The technician returns and fixes it free.

The Duntidae Promise

  • Payments held securely — money is only released when the work is signed off.
  • Verified technicians — identity and licence documents checked before accepting jobs.
  • 15-day return cover — report an issue within 15 days of completion and the technician returns to fix it free.
  • Full refund if nobody accepts — if your job expires or you cancel before a technician accepts, every cent comes back, $8.99 fee included.
  • One flat fee for posters — $8.99 per job, and no percentage is ever added to what a poster pays.
Verification covers identity and licence documents. Technicians are independent providers, responsible for the licences and insurance their trade and location require.
