Duntidae — Terms of Service

These terms are an agreement between you and Duntidae. By using the Duntidae app you accept them.

1. What Duntidae is

Duntidae is a marketplace that connects Posters (people who need work done) with independent Technicians (people who do the work). Duntidae provides the platform, payment escrow and messaging. Duntidae is not the employer of Technicians, and the contract for the actual work is between the Poster and the Technician.

2. Accounts and verification

  • You must be 18 or older and provide accurate information.
  • Technicians must complete identity/licence verification and a background check before accepting jobs.
  • You are responsible for activity on your account. You may delete your account at any time in the app; personal data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy.

3. Posting and accepting jobs

  • A job post is live for 2 hours unless accepted sooner.
  • The Poster chooses the job's location (current location, saved Home address, or a typed address). The exact address is shown only to the Technician who accepts the job.
  • Arrival policy: a Technician who accepts a job must arrive within 4 hours of accepting. If a job is outside their chosen search radius, the Technician must explicitly verify they can arrive on time; that verification is recorded.
  • Technicians share their location with the Poster only while travelling to an accepted job ("On my way").

4. Payments, escrow and fees

  • The Poster pays the job amount plus a flat $8.99 platform fee when funding a job. The full amount is held in escrow until the work is signed off.
  • A job cannot be accepted until its escrow is funded.
  • Emergency jobs: if the Emergency option is on, a surcharge up to the category maximum applies. Of the surcharge, 75% goes to the Technician and 25% to Duntidae. The base job amount is never split.
  • Payouts: when the Poster signs off a completed job, jobs under $500.00 are paid out immediately; jobs of $500.00 or more are released later the same day-cycle (after a 12-hour safety window).
  • Cancellations and expiry: if a funded job is cancelled or expires unaccepted, the job amount and any surcharge are refunded to the Poster; the $8.99 platform fee is kept by Duntidae.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe; by paying or receiving payouts you also agree to Stripe's terms.

5. The 15-day right to return

For 15 days after a job is completed, the Poster may report an issue with the work. Duntidae may review the job's proof photos, chat and other records, and may arrange a return visit, a refund, or another fair resolution at its discretion.

6. Ratings and content

Posters may rate Technicians 1–5 stars after completion. You are responsible for what you post (job descriptions, messages, photos) and must not post anything unlawful, misleading or infringing.

7. Acceptable use

You must not: use Duntidae for unlawful work; bypass escrow by arranging off-platform payment for a Duntidae job; harass other users; submit false verification documents; or interfere with the app or its servers. We may suspend or remove accounts that break these terms.

8. Liability

The work itself is performed by independent Technicians, who are responsible for its quality and safety. To the maximum extent allowed by law, Duntidae's liability is limited to the platform fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot legally be limited.
